Rules of Engagement

These rules are designed to maintain fair, structured, and realistic faction roleplay across the server. All factions are expected to understand and follow the guidelines consistently.

Common Courtesy
Failure to follow these guidelines may result in disciplinary action, including strikes and, in cases of repeated or serious violations, the potential archiving of the faction. Please be aware that the Rules of Engagement (ROE) may be updated or revised over time as circumstances and faction needs change. Faction Management will make official announcements regarding any changes to ensure all members stay informed and up to date. All members are responsible for staying informed of the latest ROE and following the most recent guidelines issued by Faction Management.

01

Attack Eligibility

Who may participate, when an incident counts as an attack, and prohibited circumstances.

8 RULES
01.1

Only faction members may participate in a faction attack. You must have been in the faction for at least 3 days before attacking with it. Violations may result in a ban and a faction strike.

01.2

A maximum of 4 members may be involved in a faction attack or hit. High-end vehicles may not be used for drills or hits.

01.3

Solo attacks are prohibited, although exceptions may be considered depending on the scenario.

01.4

A robbery is considered a faction attack only when it takes place on a faction's turf.

01.5

Situations in which a person is severely outnumbered, jumped or stripped count as a faction attack.

01.6

Attacking a faction while a court case is active is prohibited. Individuals involved may receive a permanent ban, and the faction will receive a strike.

01.7

You may not attack a faction that is on probation.

01.8

Factions may not initiate attacks during the final hour before an SSU ends.

02

Escalation & Pinpointing

Conflict must develop naturally and be supported by meaningful roleplay.

6 RULES
02.1

Before initiating a faction attack, you must have valid escalation and at least 7 meaningful negative interactions documented in your thread.

02.2

Five brawls or minor beef alone are not sufficient escalation. Poor escalation — including repeatedly fighting a rival faction solely to justify an attack — may result in a faction strike or drop removal.

02.3

To pinpoint a person, there must be personal conflict between you and the people involved. You must pinpoint an opposing faction member unless they are in a group or on their faction's turf. For example, you may identify a player you have used /mark id on.

02.4

After a negative interaction, involved members should return to their own or an ally's turf and continue block roleplay. Conflict may develop through social media, disstracks and other roleplay. Spread interactions out rather than rushing escalation.

02.5

Being on shooting terms does not mean every encounter must end in gunfire. Use realistic alternatives and do not treat every meeting as an opportunity to shoot.

02.6

Baiting a faction into attacking you while you hide on your block — for example, in a bush — to kill them is prohibited and considered play to win.

03

Combat & Weapons

Restrictions during attacks, shootouts and melee encounters.

8 RULES
03.1

If your gun jams during an attack, you must leave immediately. You may not reload to continue shooting.

03.2

Reloading is prohibited during a faction attack, whether you are attacking or defending. Driving and shooting during faction attacks are also prohibited.

03.3

Block wiping is prohibited. Only one person may be killed unless an opposing faction member fires back.

03.4

Shootouts should last no longer than 5–10 seconds. If NYPD officers are nearby or arrive, everyone must attempt to leave. Excessive chasing may result in the attack being voided and a strike being issued.

03.5

Hawking down is strictly prohibited with both guns and melee weapons. During an attack, melee pursuit must stop after a maximum of 10 seconds.

03.6

Using a lethal melee weapon, such as a knife, to initiate harm against another faction counts as a faction attack. Do not do this while on cooldown; it may result in a strike.

03.7

If carrying a broom, cane or baseball bat, you may not withdraw or use it unless you are wearing a bag or roleplay taking it from a vehicle trunk.

03.8

Weapons should not be used in every interaction simply because you have access to them. Misuse may lead to weapon removal. Attacking as soon as a cooldown expires may be treated as deathmatching and can lead to faction archive.

04

Roleplay Standards

Every attack must be part of a believable roleplay scenario.

5 RULES
04.1

All attacks must be roleplayed properly before and after they occur. This includes believable preparation and disposal of cars, clothing, gloves, masks and other evidence, as well as tinting vehicle windows where appropriate.

04.2

Drills must be realistic and creative. Examples include changing attire, washing hands, using gloves or masks, and disposing of vehicles. These actions may be demonstrated through screenshots.

04.3

Avoid a play-to-win mindset. Do not avoid roleplay or lie in /me and /do actions to gain an advantage.

04.4

If you are shot during an attack, you must roleplay your injuries even after checking in at the hospital. Failure to roleplay injuries may result in a strike.

04.5

Factions that only log in to attack or shoot will be instantly archived.This a roleplay game, not a shooting game.

05

Cooldowns & Activity

Respect retaliation windows, turf restrictions and activity expectations.

6 RULES
05.1

After any attack, there is a 7-hour cooldown before attacking another faction. The timer begins when the attack is logged in your thread.

05.2

If your faction is attacked, you may retaliate 1 hour after the initial attack.

05.3

After attacking a faction, you may not attack that same faction again unless they retaliate.

05.4

You may not visit the attacked faction's turf for 24 hours after attacking them.

05.5

You must remain active in-game for at least 1 hour after a faction attack. If killed during an attack, do not leave immediately; wait for the death timer, as EMS may arrive and save you.

05.6

Going inactive during a cooldown and returning only when you can attack may lead to faction strikes or archive. Faction Management monitors deliberate inactivity after losing firearms and activity manipulation around drop periods; strikes and archives may occur.

06

NYPD & Hospital Conduct

Police presence and post-attack hospital situations require restraint.

3 RULES
06.1

Faction attacks may not take place in areas where NYPD officers are on scene.

06.2

Factions must display cop fear. Do not shoot first at NYPD. Shooting an officer must have a significant roleplay reason; otherwise, it may result in an instant strike or faction drop removal.

06.3

If members of an opposing faction visit the hospital after an attack, do not engage in roleplay with them there. Consider using another hospital instead.

07

Evidence, Reports & Enforcement

Clipping, combat logging and reporting deadlines.

3 RULES
07.1

Every member who initiated an attack must have a clip of at least 1 minute showing their full Roblox screen. Without this, the attack may be voided and may lead to a strike or archive. Reliable clipping software is recommended; consider setting clips to 1 minute 30 seconds to ensure the full minute is captured.

07.2

Combat logging is prohibited. If caught, you may receive a permanent game ban and your faction may be archived. This includes leaving immediately after being killed; remain until the death timer has finished so EMS has a chance to respond.

07.3

Faction reports must be submitted within 24 hours of the incident.

08

RICO Bans

Serious faction-wide misconduct and ban-evasion policy.

OWNER-DECIDED

A RICO ban is a permanent game ban issued to all members of a faction, with no valid appeal permitted. The faction will also be archived. RICO bans are decided only by the game owner.

Faction toxicity

A RICO ban may be issued when a faction displays persistent toxicity toward the game, the game owner or staff, or contributes to a toxic community environment.

Out-of-character misconduct

  • Repeated OOC issues with staff or other factions.
  • Repeatedly disregarding staff or owner instructions.
  • Continued arguing with or disrespecting staff.

Ban evasion & alternate accounts

  • Members who know that someone is evading a ban or using an alternate account to bypass a ban must notify the game owner or staff immediately.
  • Sharing an account with someone banned from the game is included.
  • If staff or the owner discover the violation before it is reported, the entire faction may be permanently banned without appeal and archived.

Member accountability

If a faction contains a member banned from its Discord server, or a member is actively using an alternate account or roleplaying with the faction's acknowledgement, members must report the issue to the game owner before it is independently discovered. Failure to do so may result in a faction-wide RICO ban.

All members are expected to uphold community standards and cooperate with staff. A faction's failure to address or report serious misconduct may result in a RICO ban.
